Chefskiss 30-Day Marketing Calendar

Goal: build the first repeatable local growth loop by recruiting cooks, publishing meals, attracting customers, and measuring what converts.

Core Message

Home-cooked meals from local cooks, available tonight.

Primary Focus

Recruit cooks first, then drive customer demand in one tight local area.

Week 1: Positioning and Setup

Day 1

Pick one launch city or neighborhood cluster. Define the primary audience: cooks and nearby customers. Finalize the one-line value prop.

Day 2

Write the customer promise. Write the cook promise. Draft a short FAQ about safety, compliance, and how orders work.

Day 3

Create 3 hero message variations for the app and landing page. Create 3 social post angles. Choose 1 promotional offer for first orders.

Day 4

Build your cook outreach list of 25 to 50 prospects. Group them by cuisine, location, and quality of photos.

Day 5

Draft cook outreach messages. Draft customer launch messages. Draft a short referral pitch.

Day 6

Review your current app flow. Note any trust gaps or confusing steps. Make a list of assets still needed.

Day 7

Post a launch teaser. Send the first cook outreach batch. Record all replies and objections.

Week 2: Recruit Cooks

Day 8

Reach out to 10 more cooks. Offer a founder incentive. Ask interested cooks for photos and menus.

Day 9

Follow up on replies. Help cooks fill out onboarding. Confirm compliance and readiness.

Day 10

Publish the first 2 to 3 meals. Check meal titles, descriptions, prices, and photos. Make sure the listings look good on mobile.

Day 11

Reach out to another 10 cooks. Ask for one signature dish from each cook. Encourage strong photo uploads.

Day 12

Help cooks with compliance or profile completion. Identify blockers that keep meals from being published. Fix any onboarding confusion.

Day 13

Publish more meals. Review how meals appear in search and discovery. Create a short "available tonight" highlight list.

Day 14

Review cook progress. Aim for 5 to 10 live cooks. Prepare the customer launch push.

Week 3: Bring in Customers

Day 15

Announce the marketplace to your launch area. Post the first social content batch. Share the live menu link.

Day 16

Post meal photos and cook spotlights. Share in local groups and neighborhood channels. Ask supporters to repost.

Day 17

Run a first-order promo. Direct traffic to discover/search. Watch which cuisines get clicks.

Day 18

Share a customer trust message. Explain food safety, verification, and compliance. Keep the message simple and local.

Day 19

Send the marketplace to any warm leads. Ask friends and early users to try one meal. Capture feedback on search and checkout.

Day 20

Post a short video showing how to order. Emphasize the "tonight" use case. Highlight variety and local cooks.

Day 21

Review first customer behavior. See where users click and where they stop. Adjust the promo or message if needed.

Week 4: Tighten the Loop

Day 22

Ask every customer what made them order. Ask what almost stopped them. Record responses in one place.

Day 23

Ask every cook what was confusing. Ask what helped them publish meals fastest. Note any missing features.

Day 24

Review funnel metrics. Track search, meal click, checkout start, and order completion. Identify the weakest step.

Day 25

Improve the weakest step. Rewrite confusing copy if needed. Tighten the trust messaging.

Day 26

Do another cook outreach round. Share early wins with prospects. Ask for referrals from current cooks.

Day 27

Run a second customer promo if needed. Share one strong meal photo per post. Keep the tone local and specific.

Day 28

Review the best-performing cuisine or dish. Double down on the strongest category. Cut anything that is not converting.

Day 29

Check launch readiness. Confirm cook supply, customer interest, and order flow. Write down the next growth experiment.

Day 30

Summarize results. Decide what to repeat next month. Set the next 30-day growth target.

Weekly Metrics

  • New cooks onboarded
  • Meals published
  • Search-to-click rate
  • Checkout starts
  • Orders completed
  • Repeat orders

Recommended Focus

  1. Recruit cooks first.
  2. Make the menus look strong.
  3. Drive local customer traffic.
  4. Measure what actually converts.
  5. Improve the weakest step every week.
